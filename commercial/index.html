New Restaurant Optional Method — NEC 220.88

Complete every applicable load section below. The calculator totals the connected electrical loads, includes both heating and cooling, and applies one NEC 220.88 demand formula. Standard Method demand factors and a separate “larger result” comparison are not applied.

For a mixed-occupancy building, use this method only for a feeder or service that supplies the restaurant’s total load. Calculate other occupancy loads separately and combine them under the applicable NEC rules.

For elevators, welders, commercial dryers, multioutlet assemblies and similar specialized loads, calculate the demand under the applicable NEC article and enter that final demand load here. These entries are added once at 100% and are identified separately on the report.
